## How the Process Works

1. **Book** — Schedule 2–3 days in advance by calling (661) 862-2372. Same-day sometimes available.
2. **Inspection** — Brian inspects the vehicle at your location before starting.
3. **Detail** — Brian performs the service using professional tools and products. Typically 2–4 hours.
4. **Walkthrough** — Brian does a walkthrough with you before leaving. Touch-ups handled on the spot.
