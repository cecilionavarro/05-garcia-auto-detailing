## About Brian Garcia

On Demand Detailing (formerly Garcia Auto Detailing) was founded in 2020 by Brian Garcia in Bakersfield, CA.

Brian started detailing his own car — just wanting to keep it clean and comfortable. Friends and family noticed the results and started asking him to work on their vehicles. Word spread, referrals kept coming in, and what began as a personal passion grew into a business.

Brian started with just his Corolla and a few basic tools, driving from job to job. Over time, he reinvested every dollar he could into upgrading equipment — from the Corolla to an SUV, and now to a fully equipped professional van. Today, he brings all the professional tools and experience needed to give every vehicle the care it deserves.

## What Makes On Demand Detailing Different

- **Solo operator:** Brian personally handles every job. You get his full attention on every detail — every corner, every hidden area.
